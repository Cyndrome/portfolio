About Me

Hello! I'm Mashrafi Mohammad, a second-year Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Dhaka, studying on a merit-based OIC partial scholarship. Before university I spent two years as a graphic designer, so I look at software from two sides: the logic that makes it run, and the design that makes it feel right to use.

These days I'm active in IUTCS, solving problems on Codeforces, LeetCode and AtCoder, and publishing essays on Substack. My latest build was the backend for CareerPilot, an AI job-search platform, at the IUTCS Codesprint 2026 hackathon. From here I'm chasing more shipped projects and real hands-on experience.
